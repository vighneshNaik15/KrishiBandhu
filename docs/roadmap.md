# Development Roadmap

The current source audit, phase sequence, priorities, and acceptance gates are maintained in [implementation-roadmap.md](implementation-roadmap.md). That document supersedes this file’s former marketplace-only phase plan.

KrishiBandhu is being delivered in small, verified verticals: farmer/farm/crop foundation; safe AI disease detection; labour profiles and applications; product listings and buyer orders; farm services; weather and AI advisory; government scheme discovery; then platform operations and launch hardening.

Do not start a later phase until the current phase’s acceptance gate is met. Preserve existing routes and dependencies.
